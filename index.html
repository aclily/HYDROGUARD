A wristband that notices first

A resident wristband that checks temperature, heart rate and hydration every 2 hours, so staff catch early signs of a UTI or dehydration.

What it does
  • Checks every 2 hours. Temperature, heart rate and a hydration index, scored 0 to 9.
  • Opens their door. Tap the band on the door reader. No keys to lose.
  • Shows where they are. Lounge, garden, dining room or their own room.
